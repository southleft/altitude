<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Crafting a New Chapter in Home Bidding</h2>
<p>At Southleft, we were entrusted with the monumental task of developing a comprehensive platform for <a href="https://better.com/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Better.com</a> that would allow users to bid on homes, explore mortgage options, and delve into insurance offerings. The project was multi-layered, involving real estate listings, mortgage API integration, and insurance functionalities. The stakes were high as Better.com was on the brink of an IPO, and this platform was envisioned to be a cornerstone in adding value to their market presence.</p>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Navigating a Labyrinth in Record Time</h2>
<p>The primary challenge was the exceedingly tight deadline set against the backdrop of Better.com’s forthcoming IPO. The client aimed to diversify and enrich their service offerings to bolster their market valuation. The project was complex, involving multiple facets from real estate listings to intricate mortgage calculations. Adding to the complexity was the dynamic nature of the project requirements, which were continually evolving, making the target ever-shifting.</p>
</div>
</div>
</div>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>Agility Meets Ingenuity</strong></h2>
<p>Understanding the urgency and fluidity of the project requirements, we adopted an agile approach. We broke the project into granular tasks and distributed them among our specialized team members. To expedite development, we strategically chose off-the-shelf solutions like <a href="https://www.airtable.com/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Airtable</a> for database management. Airtable’s user-friendly interface allowed even non-technical team members at Better.com to easily manipulate data, saving significant time.</p>
<blockquote class="wp-block-quote is-layout-flow wp-block-quote-is-layout-flow">
<p>I discovered and liked Airtable through this project. Its simplicity and user-friendly nature made it a great choice for a fast-paced project like this.</p>
<cite>–Justin Young, Senior Front-End Developer</cite></blockquote>
<p>To further streamline the process, we used a monorepo structure, allowing us to share components and themes across different parts of the project. The project was built using <a href="https://nextjs.org/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Next.js</a> and <a href="https://react.dev/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">React</a>, enabling rapid development and deployment. We also implemented <a href="https://oauth.net/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">OAuth</a> for authentication and used <a href="https://formspree.io/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Formspree</a> for email functionalities.</p>
<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>Key Technologies/Tools Used:</strong></h2>
<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li><strong><a href="https://www.airtable.com/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Airtable</a></strong>: For its user-friendly database management capabilities.</li>
<li><strong><a href="https://nextjs.org/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Next.js</a></strong>: For its efficiency in front-end development.</li>
<li><strong><a href="https://react.dev/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">React</a></strong>: For its robustness in building UI components.</li>

<li><strong><a href="https://formspree.io/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Formspree</a></strong>: For seamless email functionalities.</li>
</ul>

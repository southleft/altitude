<h2 class="wp-block-heading">A Design System for Global Faith</h2>
<p>The <a href="https://www.adventist.org/">Seventh-day Adventist Church</a> needed a cohesive yet flexible design system for their diverse digital landscape. Together with design agencies <a href="https://superfriendly.com/">Superfriendly</a> and <a href="https://www.typesandsymbols.com/">Types &amp; Symbols</a>, we developed <a href="https://alps.adventist.io/v3/">ALPS</a> and even took it a step further by creating a WordPress theme to simplify website development for Adventist organizations</p>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>The Need for Unity and Customization</strong></h2>
<p>With thousands of unique institutions under its umbrella, the Adventist Church faced the challenge of maintaining brand consistency while allowing room for customization.</p>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Two Iterations and a WordPress Theme: Our Multi-Faceted Approach</h2>
<p>Our first collaboration with Superfriendly gave birth to ALPS. In our second venture with Types &amp; Symbols, we expanded ALPS into a more comprehensive system. Southleft’s role was critical in building out all the components and templates based on agency designs. We also developed a WordPress theme based on ALPS, making it even easier for organizations to launch their websites.</p>

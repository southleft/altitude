<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Unifying a Legacy of Faith</h2>
<p>We were tasked with developing a new website for <a href="https://adventistreview.org/">Adventist Review</a>, merging their existing Adventist Review and Adventist World sites into a single, cohesive platform. Our mission was to create an MVP version of the site based on provided designs, while also managing the complex process of content migration from two separate sources.</p>
<figure class="wp-block-image size-full"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" width="1311" height="991" src="https://southleft.pages.dev/media/adventistreview.org_-2-1.webp" alt="" class="wp-image-2449"><figcaption class="wp-element-caption">New Adventist Review home page</figcaption></figure>
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Bridging the Past and Present</h2>
<p>The main challenge was to create a modern, user-friendly website that respected the rich history of Adventist Review while meeting the needs of today’s digital audience. We needed to seamlessly integrate content from two separate sites, ensure compatibility with various media types including PDF magazines, and implement features like search functionality and email integration.</p>

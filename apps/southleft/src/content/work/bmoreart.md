<p>Southleft collaborated with <a href="https://www.amandabuck.com/">Studio of Amanda Buck</a> to give <a href="https://bmoreart.com/">BmoreArt</a>, a vital part of Baltimore’s art scene, a digital presence as dynamic and unique as the art world it represents. With intricate front-end development and thoughtful content migration, we helped transition the magazine from a generic template to a visually compelling, user-friendly platform.</p>

<h3>Navigating the Digital Canvas: Challenges</h3>
<p>BmoreArt needed to move beyond its templated origins and rise to the challenge of its expansive growth, especially given the logistical complexity of migrating 12+ years of rich, diverse content.</p>
</div>
</div>
<div class="c-split-media-content__copy-block" data-group="2">
<div class="o-rich-text c-split-media-content__rich-text">
<h3>A Masterpiece in Progress: Our Solution</h3>
<p>We joined hands with Studio of Amanda Buck, specializing in front-end development, WordPress customization, and the careful migration of over a decade’s worth of art and culture content.</p>
</div>
</div>
<div class="c-split-media-content__copy-block" data-group="3">
<div class="o-rich-text c-split-media-content__rich-text">
<h3>Brushstrokes &amp; Code: Design and Development Details</h3>
<p>Amanda Buck provided the artistic direction and design system, which served as our blueprint for front-end development. We ensured that each design element and interactive feature effectively contributed to a seamless user experience.</p>
</div>
</div>
<div class="c-split-media-content__copy-block" data-group="4">
<div class="o-rich-text c-split-media-content__rich-text">
<h3>Transferring a Legacy: Content Migration</h3>
<p>Content migration was a herculean task due to the site’s extensive article history. We painstakingly migrated, sanitized, and restructured the content to align with the new design architecture.</p>
</div>
</div>
</div>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>More Than a Gallery: Impact &amp; Revenue Stream</strong></h2>
<p>Post-launch, BmoreArt witnessed a noticeable boost in web traffic. The new Shopify store we integrated has served as a vital financial lifeline during the COVID-19 pandemic.</p>

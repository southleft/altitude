<h2 class="wp-block-heading">We Go WAY back</h2>
<p>When <a href="https://www.outsideonline.com/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Outside Magazine</a> first reached out, their website was static and non-responsive. This was back in 2014. They needed a modern, responsive design, and a migration to a new CMS (<a href="https://www.drupal.org/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Drupal</a>, later <a href="https://wordpress.org/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">WordPress</a>). The challenge also extended to incorporating various acquired publications, each with its unique brand identity.</p>
<figure class="wp-block-image size-full o-browser"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" width="1851" height="1093" src="https://southleft.pages.dev/media/www.outsideonline.com_culture_.webp" alt="" class="wp-image-405"><figcaption class="wp-element-caption">OutsideOnline.com category page displaying user profile menu</figcaption></figure>
<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>Building a Responsive and High-Performing Website</strong></h2>
<p>We embarked on a transformative journey with Outside Magazine, turning their static site into a mobile-first, responsive platform. Collaboratively with <a href="https://digitalbureau.com/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Digital Bureau</a>, we re-platformed the site to Drupal and later to a complex multisite parent-child WordPress theming system, allowing seamless integration of multiple brands under Outside Inc.</p>

<h2>Crafting a Flexible Component System</h2>
<p><a href="https://dschool.stanford.edu/" target="">Stanford d.school’s website</a> redesign aimed to reflect its dynamic and innovative spirit. Collaborating with one of our partner agencies, <a href="https://firebellydesign.com/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Firebelly</a> – the creative agency responsible for design and CMS integration, Southleft focused on developing a robust front-end architecture. Utilizing <a href="https://fractal.build/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Fractal</a>, we built a comprehensive component library that allowed for rapid development and seamless integration with the CMS.</p>

<h2>Navigating Unique Design Challenges</h2>
<p>The d.school’s distinctive visual identity presented challenges, including unconventional layouts and a unique grid structure. Our team meticulously adapted components to align with these designs, ensuring consistency and responsiveness across devices. The integration of animations and an elaborate theme switcher enhanced user engagement, bringing the d.school’s vibrant ethos to the digital realm.</p>

<h2>Bottleneck Blues</h2>
<p><a href="https://liquor.com" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Liquor.com</a> was looking to evolve from an affiliate-link driven model, which made up a third of their revenue. They were also eager to start selling their own branded goods. This new platform was the answer to both.</p>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading">A Spirited Approach</h2>
<p>Given the fast-paced nature of the project, we opted for a lean and agile approach. We bypassed traditional steps like wireframing and went almost straight to in-browser design and development to ensure a speedy and effective launch.</p>

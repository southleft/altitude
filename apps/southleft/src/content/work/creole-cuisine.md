<p>With over 30 restaurants under its umbrella, <a href="https://creolecuisine.com/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Creole Cuisine Restaurant Concepts</a> needed a digital presence as diverse and dynamic as its culinary offerings. <h2 class="wp-block-heading">One Theme, Many Flavors</h2>
<p>The primary challenge was designing a versatile layout to accommodate over 30 different sites. We had to address issues like varying logo dimensions—some tall, others wide. The goal was to create a flexible and modern multi-theme that could be tailored to the unique content and branding of each of the 30+ restaurants.</p>
<p>Additionally, managing a digital presence for a conglomerate of over 30 restaurants is no small feat. The platform had to be user-friendly for the small team at Creole Cuisine Corporation, who would manage all the sites. The theme needed to be easily adjustable through the WordPress dashboard, allowing for a high degree of customization without the need for extensive technical skills.</p>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Flexibility by Design</h2>
<p>Our first step was to deeply understand the specific needs and objectives of Creole Cuisine. With this insight, we shifted our focus to problem-solving within the designs, translating Creole’s requirements into a limited set of highly flexible, modular components. The project was executed on <a href="https://wordpress.org/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">WordPress</a> with designs crafted in <a href="https://www.figma.com/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Figma</a>. We used <a href="https://www.advancedcustomfields.com/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Advanced Custom Fields</a> (ACF) in WordPress for easy manipulation of site components, crafting a multi-theme that Creole Cuisine staff and restaurant managers could tailor to their specific needs. We also developed separate designs for Creole Cuisine’s main marketing site and their special events offerings.</p>

<p><a href="https://www.apqc.org/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">APQC (American Productivity &amp; Quality Center)</a> is a non-profit organization known for its expertise in benchmarking, knowledge management, measurement, and process improvement across various industries. Their primary focus is on enhancing organizational efficiency and effectiveness by providing research, benchmarks, best practices, and process improvement tools. APQC serves a diverse range of industries including healthcare, education, manufacturing, financial services, and energy.</p>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Project Overview</h2>
<p>The project entailed a major overhaul of APQC.org’s outdated website. Southleft was brought in by <a href="https://www.happycog.com/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Happy Cog</a>, alongside contract designer <a href="https://www.chriscashdollar.com/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Christopher Cashdollar</a> and contract Drupal developer <a href="https://alphex.com/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Duran Goodyear</a>, to assist with Drupal theming and front-end development. The project spanned nearly a year, involving complete re-platforming, rewriting, re-engineering the content model, and building out the extensive website.</p>
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Challenges</h2>
<p>The primary challenge was the sheer size and complexity of the site, driven by APQC’s core business of research and metrics. The project required a sophisticated content model to capture and present a vast array of data in accessible formats. This included implementing robust search functionalities, tags, and taxonomies. Furthermore, the site featured tiered membership subscription services, necessitating intricate access control to ensure members could view content and tools appropriate to their subscription level.</p>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Solution Strategy</h2>
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Approach &amp; Planning:</h3>
<p>The project was approached with meticulous planning and strategy, focusing on the intricate content model required by APQC.org. Southleft, in collaboration with Happy Cog, established detailed development workflows to maintain synchronicity throughout the project. Regular communication was a key element, ensuring that all aspects of the project were aligned and nothing was overlooked.</p>

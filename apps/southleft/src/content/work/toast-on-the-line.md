<h2>Cooking up the Front-End</h2>
<p>Working closely with design agency <a href="https://superfriendly.com/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">SuperFriendly</a>, we utilized <a href="https://patternlab.io/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Pattern Lab</a> and <a href="https://atomicdesign.bradfrost.com/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">atomic design</a> methodologies to build a responsive, user-friendly front-end. This collaborative effort included integration with Toast’s in-house development team for CMS capabilities.</p>

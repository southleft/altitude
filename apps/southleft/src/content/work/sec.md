<h2 class="wp-block-heading">A Digital Playground for Sustainable Energy Enthusiasts</h2>
<p>We began by creating a full Figma mockup to visualize the layout and design elements. Custom animations, including a rotating globe and zooming effects, were built using Google Earth and were incorporated to enhance user engagement. Key technologies/tools utilized included fractal as a pattern library for them incorporated into a Drupal site.</p>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading">A Dynamic Platform Promoting Sustainable Energy Initiatives</h2>
<p>The redesigned landing page and interactive database successfully presented SEC&#8217;s projects with clarity and impact. The inclusion of relevant imagery and detailed project overviews contributed to a more immersive user experience. Our collaboration with AIChE ensured alignment with the client&#8217;s vision and objectives.</p>

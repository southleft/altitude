<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>Navigating a Complex Transition</strong></h2>
<p class="o-browser">In collaboration with <a href="https://www.lullabot.com/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Lullabot</a>, we faced the complex task of replatforming IBM’s Investor Relations site from their old content management system. As a crucial channel for investor communications, any change had to be implemented with utmost caution to maintain trust and data integrity. Additionally, this project required the migration from IBM’s older design system, Northstar, to their new, more streamlined and scalable <a href="https://carbondesignsystem.com/">Carbon design system</a>.</p>
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large" id="o-browser"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" width="1400" height="987" src="https://southleft.pages.dev/media/www.ibm_.com_investor_events.webp" alt="IBM Investor Relations interior page" class="wp-image-714"></figure>
<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>A Multi-Agency Collaboration</strong></h2>
<p>Our team of front-end developers joined forces with Lullabot, who took on the roles of UX design, back-end and module development, and content modeling. Southleft focused on the intricacies of front-end development and Drupal theming, intricately weaving the new Carbon design system into the new platform. We closely coordinated with IBM’s in-house team of designers and developers to execute this multifaceted project, ensuring that the final product would not only meet but exceed expectations.</p>
